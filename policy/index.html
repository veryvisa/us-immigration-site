出生公民权行政令（第 14160 号）被最高法院判违宪

Executive Order 14160 / Trump v. Barbara (U.S. Supreme Court)

2025 年 1 月 20 日的第 14160 号行政令主张：父母在美非法或临时居留所生子女不是美国公民。最高法院 2026 年 6 月 30 日在 Trump v. Barbara 案判决：这些儿童属于「受美国管辖」，出生即为美国公民。

影响谁：父母在美非法或临时居留（含持学生、工作、旅游签证）时在美出生的儿童

“Argued April 1, 2026—Decided June 30, 2026 [...] On January 20, 2025, President Trump issued Executive Order No. 14160, titled Protecting the Meaning and Value of American Citizenship. [...] Held: Children born in the United States to parents unlawfully or temporarily present are “subject to the jurisdiction” of the United States and are citizens at birth under the Fourteenth Amendment’s Citizenship Clause.”

相关类别：B-1/B-2 F-1 H-1B
